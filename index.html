Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is the resume builder free?

Yes. You can build and download your resume at no cost.

Can I download a PDF?

Yes. Choose Download PDF on the last step.

Can I download a Word (DOCX) file?

Yes. Download DOCX gives you a file you can keep editing in Word.

Can freshers create resumes?

Yes. Pick Fresher in the profile step and skip work experience. Add projects, internships and skills instead.

Is registration required?

No. There is no sign-up. Open the builder and start typing.

Is my personal information secure?

Your resume is saved only in your own browser and is not uploaded to our servers. The only exception is the optional Improve with AI button, which sends just the text you are improving. Clear your browser data to remove your saved resume.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Resume Creative does not have user accounts and does not upload your resume. Everything you type, including an optional photo, is stored only in your own browser on your device. We cannot see it.

To create PDF and Word files, this site loads open-source libraries (html2pdf.js, FileSaver.js and docx) from public content delivery networks. Like any web request, this lets those networks see your IP address and browser details.

The optional Improve with AI button sends only the text in that field, plus your job title and experience level, to our server, which passes it to an AI provider (Anthropic) to write a suggestion. Our service does not store it, and the provider handles it under its own terms. Use the button only for content you are comfortable sharing.

Your cookie choices are saved in your browser. You can change them anytime from Cookie Preferences in the footer. To delete your resume and settings, clear this site’s data in your browser.
